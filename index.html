Guillermo Droppelmann, PhD

Guillermo Droppelmann is a Clinical Researcher and PhD in Health Sciences (Summa Cum Laude) with postdoctoral training in Engineering. He is currently pursuing a B.S. in Physics, deepening the mathematical and physical foundations of his work.

He applies clinical practice and data science, with a research focus on machine and deep learning applied to musculoskeletal imaging and sports analytics, and on multimodal AI modeling of signals and biomarkers in general population and cancer patients.

He currently serves as Deputy Head of Research at Clínica MEDS and as Lecturer in the PhD Program in Industrial Engineering at PUCV.

Research areas

  • Machine learning and deep learning applied to medical imaging
  • Health status with a focus on cardiometabolic diseases and cancer
  • Clinical research methodology, biostatistics and research ethics
  • Modality-Aware Sports Analytics
